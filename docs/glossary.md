# Glossary

> Banking and fraud-domain terms used throughout the platform.
> For engineers coming from a non-banking background; for analysts
> coming from a non-engineering background.

## A

**AML (Anti-Money Laundering)**
Regulations that require financial institutions to detect and report
suspicious activity. In the U.S., the Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) and
FinCEN are the primary frameworks.

**API (Application Programming Interface)**
A way for two software systems to talk. The Identity Intelligence
Platform exposes a REST API that the Streamlit UI and external
integrations consume.

**Audit Log**
A chronological, append-only record of every action the system took.
For fraud detection, every decision is audited. See ADR-0003.

**Authenticate (in payments)**
Verifying that the person initiating a payment is who they say they
are. Voice biometrics, face ID, and out-of-band callbacks are all
forms of authentication.

## B

**Beneficiary**
The person or entity receiving a payment.

**BLOCK**
A decision action. The payment is held and a case is opened. The
strongest response; used when score ≥ 0.80.

**BSA (Bank Secrecy Act)**
The U.S. law that requires financial institutions to report
suspicious activity. Forms include the SAR (Suspicious Activity
Report) and the CTR (Currency Transaction Report).

**BIC (Bank Identifier Code)**
The SWIFT code for a bank. Equivalent to ABA routing for U.S.
domestic wires.

## C

**CASE**
A case is opened when a payment is `BLOCK`ed or `REVIEW`ed. An
investigator works the case to a resolution (confirmed fraud or
false positive).

**CDD (Customer Due Diligence)**
The process of verifying a customer's identity and assessing their
risk. The Identity Intelligence Platform's per-person `risk_score`
is part of CDD.

**Cross-border**
A payment that crosses a national border. Higher risk than
domestic; subject to additional compliance checks.

## D

**Deepfake**
A piece of synthetic media (audio, video, image) generated by a
generative AI model to impersonate a real person. The platform
detects the *behavioral pattern* of a deepfake-driven fraud
attempt, not the media itself.

**Decision**
The output of the real-time scorer: `ALLOW`, `REVIEW`, or `BLOCK`.

**DFS (Department of Financial Services)**
The New York State regulator. The DFS Part 500 (cybersecurity)
applies to any bank operating in New York.

## E

**ECB (European Central Bank)**
Issues guidance on AI in banking; the 2025-2027 framework includes
mandatory controls on high-value wires.

**EFS (European Federation of Statisticians)**
Not a banking term; included for completeness on international
fraud statistics.

**EU AI Act (high-risk provisions)**
Effective mid-2026. Requires explainability and human-in-the-loop
for AI decisions in financial services. The platform's hybrid
scoring + audit log are designed to comply.

## F

**FATF (Financial Action Task Force)**
The international body that sets AML/CFT standards. Their "grey list"
and "black list" of high-risk jurisdictions are encoded in the
platform's `COUNTRY_RISK` dictionary.

**FedNow**
The U.S. Federal Reserve's instant payment service. Distinct from
FedWire (the older large-value wire system).

**FedWire**
The U.S. Federal Reserve's real-time gross settlement (RTGS) system.
Used for large-value and time-critical wires.

**Feature**
A numeric input to the ML model. The platform uses 19 features per
event (see data-dictionary.md §5).

**Final Score**
`max(xgb_score, rule_score)`. The single number that drives the
ALLOW/REVIEW/BLOCK decision. See ADR-0002.

**Fraud**
A payment initiated with intent to deceive for financial gain. The
platform distinguishes four kinds: `STANDARD_FRAUD`,
`DEEPFAKE_ATTACK`, `MONEY_MULE`, `SYNTHETIC_IDENTITY`.

**FRONTIER+**
The Singapore-Hong Kong police cooperation framework. Used in the
documented Singapore deepfake case (Mar 2025) to recover the
$499K wire.

**FS-AI RMF (Federal AI Risk Management Framework)**
The U.S. Treasury's framework (Feb 2026) for AI in financial
services. The platform's audit log is shaped by its traceability,
explainability, and version-pinning requirements.

## G

**GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)**
EU data protection law. The platform stores PII encrypted at rest
and supports data-subject deletion via the `purge_actor()` API
(Tier 2).

## H

**HKD (Hong Kong Dollar)**
The currency of Hong Kong. The Arup pattern transferred the $25.6M
loss to HKD-denominated mule accounts.

**Hybrid Scoring**
A scoring strategy that combines ML with deterministic rules. The
platform uses `final_score = max(xgb, rules)`. See ADR-0002.

## I

**Identity Graph**
A graph database (the platform uses Neo4j) that connects people,
accounts, devices, IPs, and other entities. Used for entity
resolution and mule ring detection.

**IBAN (International Bank Account Number)**
The international standard for bank account numbers. Used in SEPA
payments.

**Investigator**
A human analyst who works cases. The platform is designed to make
their work efficient: one click to see the event, the features,
the rules that fired, and the related graph neighborhood.

**ISO 20022**
The international standard for financial messaging. Replacing
SWIFT MT messages; the platform's event schema is ISO 20022-aware.

## K

**KYC (Know Your Customer)**
The process of verifying a customer's identity. The platform
ingests `KYC_UPDATE` events when a person's status changes
(VERIFIED, LIMITED, REJECTED, etc.).

## L

**Latency**
The time from event arrival to decision emission. Target: p99 <
200ms. Current measured: p99 = 0.04ms (heuristic) / ~5ms (with
XGBoost + SHAP).

**Likelihood Ratio**
In a fraud-detection context, the ratio of fraud probability given
the observed features to the base rate. Used in calibration.

## M

**Mule (Money Mule)**
A person whose account is used (often unwittingly) to receive and
forward fraud proceeds. Mule rings are network-level patterns
detected by the graph layer.

**MLflow**
The open-source ML lifecycle platform. The platform uses MLflow
for experiment tracking and model registry. Hosted on EC2 t3.micro
in the free tier.

## O

**OFAC SDN (Office of Foreign Assets Control - Specially Designated Nationals)**
The U.S. sanctions list. The platform's R010 rule does a fuzzy
match on beneficiaries against this list.

**OpenLineage**
The open standard for data lineage. The platform emits OpenLineage
events for every decision.

**Out-of-band**
A communication channel separate from the one being used. Out-of-band
authentication is the gold standard for high-value wires: the bank
calls the CFO back on a known phone number to confirm.

## P

**Payment Rail**
The network that carries the payment. The platform supports
SWIFT_ISO20022, FedNow, RTP, Card, ACH, SEPA, Wire, and Internal.

**PEP (Politically Exposed Person)**
A person who holds a prominent public function. PEPs require
enhanced due diligence. The platform's `Person` model has a
`pep_status` flag.

**Person**
A natural person in the identity graph. Distinct from `Account`,
which is the bank-side entity.

## R

**Recall**
The fraction of actual frauds the model catches. Target: ≥ 0.95
for the deepfake class.

**REVIEW**
A decision action. The payment is queued for human review. Used
when 0.50 ≤ score < 0.80.

**RTP (Real-Time Payments)**
The U.S. real-time payment network operated by The Clearing House.
Akin to FedNow but private-sector.

**Rule Engine**
The deterministic counterpart to the XGBoost scorer. 10 default
rules in `configs/rules.yaml`.

## S

**SAR (Suspicious Activity Report)**
A filing to FinCEN when a bank suspects a transaction may involve
funds derived from illegal activity. Generated by the investigator
in the case-management UI; not generated by the model itself.

**SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area)**
The EU's integrated euro-payment system.

**SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations)**
A method for explaining ML model outputs. The platform computes
SHAP values for every ML-scored event.

**Signatory**
A person authorized to initiate payments on behalf of an entity.
Larger amounts typically require multiple signatories.

**Streaming**
A data processing pattern where events are processed as they
arrive, not in batches. The platform is streaming-first; batch
processing is for offline training.

**SWIFT (Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication)**
The international bank-to-bank messaging network. The platform
supports SWIFT ISO 20022 messages.

## T

**Threshold**
A score cutoff that determines the action. BLOCK ≥ 0.80, REVIEW
≥ 0.50, ALLOW < 0.50.

**Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3**
The platform's roadmap phases. Tier 1 (this repo) is the MVP.
Tier 2 is production-hardening. Tier 3 is research. See
`docs/tier2_roadmap.md`.

**Tokenization**
Replacing a sensitive value (e.g., a card number) with a
non-sensitive equivalent. The platform does not tokenize today
but the schema reserves a `payment_token` field for Tier 2.

**Trust Chain**
The series of signals (login, MFA, voice biometric, callback)
that establish that a person is who they claim to be. The
deepfake attacks target the trust chain, not the transaction.

## V

**Velocity**
The rate at which an actor transacts. The platform's R001 rule
flags more than 3 wires per hour from the same account.

**Vulnerability**
A weakness in a system. The platform's security model documents
its attack surface in `docs/SECURITY.md`.

## W

**Walkthrough**
The end-to-end demo script at `examples/walkthrough.py`. Runs the
full pipeline on synthetic data without Docker.

**Wire (Wire Transfer)**
A real-time, irrevocable bank-to-bank transfer. The Arup pattern
used 15 wires totaling $25.6M; the Singapore pattern used 3
wires totaling $1.9M.

## X

**XGBoost (eXtreme Gradient Boosting)**
A gradient-boosted decision-tree library. The platform's primary
ML model. See ADR-0007.
